Privacy Policy

Movie Plus (com.movieplus.moviesandtv.app) · Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Movie Plus. This Privacy Policy informs you how our mobile application handles user information, device storage, third-party services, and data privacy rights.

Summary:

  • No Account Required: Movie Plus does not require personal registration, email signup, or login.
  • Local Storage: Your watchlist, search history, and settings are saved locally on your phone and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Third-Party Integrations: We use industry-standard Google services (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config) and the public TMDB API.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Movie Plus prioritizes offline privacy. The following data is stored solely in local app storage using Android Room Database and DataStore Preferences:

Category Purpose Storage Location
Watchlist Saves movie titles, posters, release years, and personal ratings so you can view them anytime offline. Local device storage (SQLite/Room)
Search History Recent search queries for quick auto-complete. Local DataStore
App Preferences Tracks onboarding completion, theme preferences, and review timing. Local DataStore
Cached Media Image cache (posters & backdrops) to ensure fast rendering and reduce network usage. Local app cache (Coil)

All local data can be permanently deleted at any time by clearing the app data in Android System Settings or by uninstalling the application.

2. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

Movie Plus integrates with third-party software development kits (SDKs) to provide movie information, advertisements, and crash monitoring:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

Movie Plus is a free application supported by advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including Google Advertising ID), coarse location derived from IP addresses, and ad performance metrics to serve relevant ads.

  • In regions where required (such as the EEA, UK, and California), users are presented with a consent form on initial launch.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime via your phone settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete or Reset Advertising ID).
  • Learn more: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

B. Google Firebase Analytics & Remote Config

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated, anonymous user engagement metrics (such as daily active users, popular genres, and screen navigation). Firebase Remote Config is used to dynamically update non-sensitive configuration parameters (such as ad frequency intervals).

  • Analytics data is anonymized and never linked to personally identifiable information.
  • Learn more: Firebase Privacy Policy.

C. Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics collects non-identifiable crash logs, device hardware models, and operating system versions when unexpected errors occur, assisting us in resolving stability issues.

D. The Movie Database (TMDB) API

Movie metadata, synopses, cast information, and posters are retrieved via the TMDB API. Search queries are sent directly to TMDB to fetch matching titles. Movie Plus does not send TMDB your personal identity or private watchlist items.

Notice: This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
Learn more: TMDB Privacy Policy.

E. YouTube (Official Movie Trailers)

Trailer playback uses official YouTube trailer links. Playing a trailer interacts with the YouTube player or app governed by Google Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

Permission Usage
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to fetch movie details, load artwork, stream trailers, and display advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Movie Plus is not aimed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to third-party services through our app, please contact us immediately so we can assist in its deletion.

5. User Rights & Data Control (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Since we do not host user accounts, all your data resides directly on your device.
  • Right to Deletion: You can wipe all stored data instantly by selecting Clear Data in your device's Application Manager or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can manage ad preferences in Google settings or reset your advertising identifier at any time.

6. Content & Streaming Disclaimer

Movie Plus is purely an informational discovery and editorial review application. Movie Plus does not host, stream, pirate, or download full-length copyrighted video files. All video playback functionality is strictly restricted to official promotional trailers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of the updated terms.
